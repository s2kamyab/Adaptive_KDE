\subsection{Evaluation mechanism and benchmark functions}
We choose Mean Integrated Squared Error (MISE) method as in (\ref{eq:ise}) as evaluation criterion 
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ise}
MISE = E||f_n-f||^2_2=E(\sum_i((f_n(x_i)-f(x_i))^2))
\end{equation}
16 one dimensional benchmark densities having different characteristics are used to evaluate the performance  proposed method in different domains. figure \ref{fig:bench} shows the shap of each benchmark density which is made of different normal PDFs with different centers and standard deviations.

# Figure captions

## figureA_conf

Figure A (confirmation counterpart). Fitted densities on a single dataset per panel, in original data units. Rows: the four diagnostic targets f0 (MW1 Gaussian), f2 (MW3 strongly skewed), f5 (MW6 bimodal), f9 (MW10 claw). Columns: N = 200 and N = 5000. All five curves in a panel are fitted to the identical observations, shown as a faint rug. Methods: ground truth (black solid); original V2 AKDE (orange dashed); the frozen finalist A6_moment_matched_m300 (blue solid); adapted SPADES, i.e. density-valid non-negative selection plus simplex refit (purple dash-dot); Sheather-Jones KDE (green dotted). Selection rule: hyperparameters chosen by 3-fold cross-validation of the held-out integrated-square score on training data only; the dictionary is pinned at 60x5 = 300 atoms for every dictionary-based method. Replicate selection was frozen before any outcome was inspected: rep 0, the first prespecified seed, not the best-looking replicate. The plotting window is the predeclared truth quantile range [0.0005, 0.9995]; it was never used in training. The largest fitted mass falling outside that window, over all curves and panels, is 0.0120, so no curve is materially truncated and no tail panel is needed; the per-curve values are in the companion CSV. No y-limit cap is applied: where the original AKDE spikes far above the truth, the spike is shown at full height. Where a reference curve is not visible it coincides with the finalist, not because it is missing. Annotations give the ISE and active support size of the original and finalist fits; complete metrics are in figures/data/figureA_conf_metrics.csv. Truth was used only for evaluation and for drawing the reference curve. Drawn from the locked Stage-C confirmation run.

## figureB_N1000

Figure B. Variability of the fitted density across 30 independent confirmation datasets at N = 1000, for the four diagnostic targets. Each panel shows the arithmetic mean fitted density (coloured line) and the pointwise 10th-90th percentile band across replicates (shaded), against the ground truth (black). These are EMPIRICAL VARIABILITY BANDS describing the spread of the estimator across datasets; they are NOT confidence bands for the true density, and at R = 30 a 10th-90th percentile band is resolved by only the 2nd and 9th order statistics, so its edges are themselves noisy. The mean of normalised densities is itself a density; the ISE of this mean curve is a different quantity from the mean ISE reported in Table form and the two must not be interchanged. Columns: original V2 AKDE, the frozen finalist, and one conventional reference. Selection rule and dictionary as in Figure A. Truth used only for evaluation and display.

## figureC

Figure C. Why the moment fit misses. Columns: f5 (MW6 bimodal) and f9 (MW10 claw). Row 1: fitted-density error f_hat(x) - f0(x) for the original V2 AKDE and the frozen finalist, at N = 500 (faint) and N = 5000 (solid), on development replicate 0. Row 2: standardised training measurement residuals (Du - y)_j divided by the estimated sampling standard deviation of y_j, plotted against measurement centre and separated by probe width (darker = wider), on a symlog scale with dotted lines at +/- 2. A narrow probe centred where the sample has no points has an estimated sampling variance that underflows to numerically zero, so its standardised residual is 0/0; those probes are DROPPED and counted in the panel rather than clipped, and the counts are also in the companion CSV (n_probes_unresolved). A probe is kept only when its effective sample count, Kish's (sum_n k_j(x_n))^2 / sum_n k_j(x_n)^2, is at least 5. Row 3: the same fits' population residual Du - y0 (open markers, y0 computed in closed form from the true density — an oracle quantity used for diagnosis only) against the training residual Du - y (crosses). Rows 2-3 together show whether a measured discrepancy that is small on its own sampling scale coexists with a large density error in row 1. Row 4: the measurement-count sweep from the V2 archive, shown PER TARGET rather than pooled over {f5, f9} as the manuscript's Table 6 does; 10 replicates per point, median ISE. Truth was used only for evaluation and for the oracle residual.

## figureD

Figure D. Accuracy and cost over the locked Stage-C confirmation (15 Marron-Wand targets, N in {200, 1000, 5000}, R = 30 independent datasets per cell, six methods). Top block: median ISE against N, one small panel per target, log-log. Any apparent slope is a DESCRIPTIVE finite-range summary over three sample sizes; no convergence rate is inferred from it. Middle: paired ISE ratios finalist / original V2 AKDE, one point per replicate, black bar at the geometric mean, dashed line at the 0.8 development objective (a proposed decision threshold, not a target the confirmation was required to meet). Bottom: median ISE against the final active support size and against cold end-to-end fit time, one point per (target, N). Selection rule: 3-fold cross-validated held-out integrated-square score, training data only; pinned 60x5 dictionary for every dictionary-based method. Truth used only for evaluation.
